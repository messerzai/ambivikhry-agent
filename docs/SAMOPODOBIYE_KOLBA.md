# Kolba investigation

## Status
Open. The exact meaning given by Denis Isay to 'Колба' has not yet been established from a primary source.

## Do not conflate
The classical Kolb experiential-learning cycle is commonly represented as experience -> reflective observation -> abstract conceptualization -> active experimentation.
The author's use of 'Колба' may be different. Until a primary source is located, the two must remain separate records.

## Research questions
1. Where does Isay first define or use the term?
2. What are the named stages?
3. What enters the cycle and what exits it?
4. Is the cycle individual, interpersonal, organizational, or AI-specific?
5. Does it claim recurrence/self-similarity across scales?
6. What observations would falsify it?
7. Is it descriptive, prescriptive, or both?

## Source priority
Telegram primary posts -> recordings/transcripts -> author articles -> independent discussion.
