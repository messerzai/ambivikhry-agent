## Required review before each reuse

1. Pin an immutable commit, release, model revision or dataset version.
2. Read the actual LICENSE file and any NOTICE, model card, dataset card, terms of use and gated-access agreement.
3. Identify separate licenses for source code, weights, datasets, pretrained tokenizers and APIs.
4. Check commercial/non-commercial limits, attribution, redistribution, modification, patent grants, copyleft and network-use clauses.
5. Add exact reused paths, modifications, dependency pins and notices to this file.
6. If terms are absent or ambiguous, do not copy. Use an independent implementation based on cited public research instead.
7. Re-run the audit before distributing binaries, checkpoints, containers or hosted services.

This is a provenance checklist, not a legal opinion. Escalate ambiguous or high-impact licensing questions for qualified review.
